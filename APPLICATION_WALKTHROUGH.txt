1. APPLICATION OVERVIEW & ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The OEM Finance Lead Portal is an enterprise-grade automotive financial 
management platform connecting Automotive OEMs, dealer distribution networks, 
and Tier-1 national banking partners/NBFCs (HDFC, SBI, ICICI, Axis, Kotak, 
Bajaj Finance). 

Key Architecture Highlights:
- Multi-Lender Parallel API Bidding: When a dealer inputs a customer lead, loan
  requests broadcast in parallel to all selected banks' Lending Origin Systems (LOS).
- Real-Time SLA Monitoring: Tracks bank response latency down to the second.
- Dual Experience: Desktop command center for dealership managers & OEM finance
  executives, paired with an integrated Mobile Field Companion App for sales
  representatives on showroom floors.
- Responsive Drawer & Navigation: Full mobile & desktop adaptive interface.

3. SCREEN-BY-SCREEN FUNCTIONAL BREAKDOWN
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCREEN 1: DASHBOARD OVERVIEW (Tab: 'dashboard')
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose: High-level financial control center for OEM managers to evaluate
pipeline health, incoming loan volumes, and lending partner conversion.

Step-by-Step Layout & User Journey:
1. Top Engine Status Banner:
   - Displays real-time status: "Algorithmic Multi-Lender Dispatch Active".
   - Provides quick action shortcuts: "Create Customer Lead Dossier" and
     "View Active Bank Bids".
2. Four Key Performance Metric Cards:
   - Total Leads (30D): Cumulative volume across 124 dealerships with +14.8% trend.
   - Approved / Sanctioned: Real-time approval count (74.2% conversion rate).
   - Avg Bank SLA Speed: Instant API performance average (14.2 seconds).
   - Disbursed GTV: Total vehicle finance volume in Crores (₹247.5 Cr).
3. Active Loan Dossiers Table (Left 8 Columns):
   - Displays incoming customer applications with ID, vehicle model, dealership,
     loan amount, and status pill (Draft, Submitted, Under Review, Approved).
   - "Compare Bids" Button: Instantly binds the selected lead and routes the user
     to the Live Offer Board.
4. Connected Bank Quick Health Panel (Right 4 Columns):
   - Displays real-time status of top banks (HDFC, ICICI, SBI, Axis, Kotak).
   - Shows base ROI rates, SLA latency, and disbursement speed type (Instant, Fast, Standard).
   - Smart Routing Engine tip indicating optimal bank profiles for salaried vs. self-employed.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCREEN 2: CREATE NEW LEAD (Tab: 'create-lead')
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose: Dealer entry wizard to capture customer KYC, vehicle requirements, and
broadcast parallel underwriting requests to selected banks.

Step-by-Step Form Sections:
1. Section 1: Customer Dossier:
   - Full Customer Name, Mobile Number, Email Address.
   - PAN Card Number (auto-uppercased font-mono for credit bureau validation).
   - Date of Birth & City/Region.
2. Section 2: Vehicle & Loan Configuration:
   - Vehicle Model: Dropdown of OEM models (Grand Vitara Hybrid, XUV700, Nexon EV, etc.).
   - Ex-Showroom Price (₹): Automated vehicle list pricing.
   - Required Loan Amount (₹): Editable loan principal.
   - Customer Down Payment (₹): Self-financed portion.
   - Dealership Origin: Assigns local dealership name.
   - Expected Delivery Date: Sets financing deadline for banks.
3. Section 3: Select Partner Banks for Parallel Bidding:
   - Grid displaying all connected banks with individual toggle cards.
   - Users can select/deselect institutions or broadcast to all 6 simultaneously.
   - Shows individual institution SLA (e.g., 10s - 45s).
4. Submission Workflow:
   - Clicking "Submit & Query Bank APIs" triggers simulated network latency.
   - Generates a unique Application ID (e.g., FL-29041).
   - Displays a success confirmation screen with direct navigation into the Live Offer Board.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCREEN 3: LIVE OFFER BOARD (Tab: 'offer-board')
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose: Side-by-side comparative bidding arena evaluating received bank offers
for an individual customer lead.

Step-by-Step Features & Controls:
1. Customer Context Bar:
   - Shows active lead name, application ID badge, credit score pill (e.g., 785 Experian Verified).
   - Displays selected car model and requested loan amount.
2. Sorting Engine Controls:
   - "Lowest ROI": Reorders cards by lowest annual percentage rate.
   - "Lowest EMI": Reorders cards by lowest monthly installment in INR.
   - "Fastest API SLA": Reorders cards by fastest turnaround time.
3. Comparative Offer Cards:
   - Visual Badges: "Best Value", "Lowest ROI & EMI", "Instant Disbursal", "Counter Offer".
   - Bank Branding: Official institution emblem, name, and response latency timestamp.
   - Primary Metrics Box: Large typography for fixed ROI (%) and monthly EMI (₹).
   - Loan Terms Breakdown: Sanctioned amount, tenure (e.g., 5 Years / 60 Months),
     processing fees, and prepayment/foreclosure terms.
4. One-Click Deal Actions:
   - "Select & Generate Sanction": Accepts the chosen offer, marks lead status as
     'Approved'/'Sanctioned', and prompts confirmation.
   - "Share with Customer": Copies a customer-formatted offer breakdown directly
     to clipboard for SMS/WhatsApp/Email dispatch.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCREEN 4: LEAD PIPELINE TRACKER (Tab: 'lead-tracker')
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose: Comprehensive operational table to monitor all active and archived
customer finance applications.

Step-by-Step Features:
1. Search & Filter Bar:
   - Real-time search query matching Application ID, Customer Name, Vehicle Model, or Dealer.
   - Horizontal status filter chips: [All, Draft, Submitted, Under Review, Approved, Sanctioned, Disbursed].
2. Pipeline Data Table:
   - Application ID: Unique reference code + creation timestamp.
   - Customer Profile: Name + phone number.
   - Vehicle & Dealership: Model name + origin branch.
   - Loan Amount: Requested loan figure + customer down payment.
   - Status Pill: Color-coded operational status (Blue, Yellow, Green, Purple).
   - Bureau Score: Credit rating validation.
3. Row Actions:
   - Eye Icon: Opens the full Lead Detail Modal (inspect KYC, change lifecycle status).
   - "Bids" Button: Quick shortcut to launch the Live Offer Board for that specific lead.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCREEN 5: CONNECTED BANK APIS (Tab: 'connected-banks')
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose: Institution relationship management screen to oversee technical health,
webhook response times, and underwriting criteria.

Step-by-Step Features:
1. Partner Institution Cards:
   - Bank Logo & Short Code badge.
   - API Status: "Direct LOS API Connected" with green pulse dot.
   - Operational Description: Underwriting specialties (e.g., EV zero-foreclosure,
     thin-file algorithms, green channel e-KYC).
   - Quantitative Metrics Grid: Average SLA speed, Base ROI, and Conversion Rate %.
   - Cumulative Financed GTV: Disbursed volume in Crores.
2. Health Check Ping:
   - Interactive button testing webhook heartbeat and returning simulated latency (124ms).
3. Connect New Partner API:
   - Header button launching the "Connect New Bank Partner" modal to configure new bank APIs.

4. MODAL DIALOGS & OVERLAYS
================================================================================

A. LEAD DETAIL DOSSIER MODAL (`LeadDetailModal.tsx`)
----------------------------------------------------
- Triggered by clicking the Eye icon on any lead in the Pipeline Tracker.
- Displays comprehensive customer dossier: PAN, DOB, contact, city, bureau score.
- Displays vehicle specs, ex-showroom price, and loan amounts.
- Lists all received bank bids for that lead.
- Direct operational actions: "Approve Lead", "Mark Sanctioned", or "Close Dossier".

B. CONNECT NEW BANK PARTNER MODAL (`AddPartnerModal.tsx`)
---------------------------------------------------------
- Triggered from the Connected Banks screen.
- Form inputs: Institution Name, Short Code, Target SLA, Base ROI %, Brand Color.
- Instantly adds the new lender to the bidding network.

C. GLOBAL DATASET EXPORT MODAL (`ExportModal.tsx`)
--------------------------------------------------
- Triggered from the top header download button or Reports screen.
- Formats: CSV, Excel (.xlsx), or JSON.
- Filter options: All Time, Today, or Last 30 Days.
- Triggers browser file download.
